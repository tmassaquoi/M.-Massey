The From Soil to Sky Training Series is a seven-course professional development program for organizational leaders who want to develop or lead youth development and mentoring programming. Each course goes deep on one framework — or a synthesis of frameworks — and applies it directly to program design, facilitation, and organizational practice.

This series is rooted in African philosophical traditions, developmental science, and decades of mentoring practice. It is designed to be studied sequentially — start with FOUNDATIONS and build from there. Every course produces work you can use immediately in your organization.

About the Designer

Tiana Massaquoi

Tiana Massaquoi is the founder of Keep Still Cares Foundation and the creator of the From Soil to Sky Program Model — a year-long youth development and mentoring program structured around a four-phase rites of passage journey (Soil → Seed → Stem → Sky). The FSTS Training Series is Tiana's contribution to the field: a complete professional development curriculum that gives organizational leaders the theoretical grounding and practical tools to build programs that heal as well as develop young people.

The series draws on Tiana's original synthesis of African cultural philosophy, developmental science, and community practice, and represents a distinct contribution to the fields of youth development, mentoring, and culturally grounded organizational leadership.
